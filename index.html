Can I get more than one service?+

Yes. You can discuss onboarding, registrations and creative work together. We’ll confirm the scope and charges for the services you choose before starting.

Do you work with cafés and small food shops?+

Yes. TrustRise Restaurant Consultant supports restaurants, cafés, hotels and small food businesses. Tell us your business type and current stage so we can discuss a suitable starting point.

Are you an official platform or government partner?+

No. TrustRise Restaurant Consultant is an independent consultancy. Zomato, Swiggy and other platform names belong to their respective owners. We provide application and coordination support; platform and government decisions remain with the relevant organisations.

What will it cost, and how long will it take?+

Fees and estimated timelines depend on the service and scope. Contact us for a quote. Government or platform charges, where applicable, will be discussed separately. Application processing and approvals depend on the relevant authority or platform.

What should I share in my first inquiry?+

Your name, business type, location and the service you’re interested in are enough to start. Please don’t send passwords, OTPs or identity documents through this inquiry form. Any documents needed later can be discussed directly.
